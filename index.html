What we found / 1 of 3

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

01

No active ads found.

Meta’s selected practice page showed no active U.S. ads. If current, nearby prospects may meet competitors first. October 20, All ads/Active filters; other pages or countries may differ. See advertiser search ↗

02

Searchers get a contact card.

The homepage has no treatment list, hours or treatment-page links. Ad and search visitors lack detail to choose your practice. October 20 browser view; possibly mid-rebuild. See homepage ↗

03

The clinician is missing.

The homepage has no doctor introduction, team photos or practitioner video. New clients cannot meet the person behind the care before deciding to trust you. October 20 page review. See homepage ↗

What we found / 2 of 3

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

04

Your reviews stay off-page.

The homepage has no reviews or case stories despite your 4.7 Google rating. Comparing clients must leave your site to find reasons to choose you. October 20 page review. See homepage ↗

05

The new-client reply time is unclear.

New clients get a Google Form; returning clients get Boulevard. No reply window is shown. Uncertainty after an inquiry can send a ready-to-book client elsewhere. See inquiry form ↗

What we found / 3 of 3

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

06

The email inbox changes.

The displayed contact inbox links to the schedule inbox. That change can make someone hesitate before sending an inquiry. Both may work; this is a label mismatch, not proven delivery failure. See homepage ↗

07

4.5 seconds in the test.

Homepage main content appeared after 4.5 seconds in our phone simulation. Visitors may leave before seeing your care. October 20, one slow-4G test; not full-page loading or every visitor’s wait. See mobile test ↗
