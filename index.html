02 / What could make the next inquiry harderPublic-page review / October 20, 2026

A first-time visitor needs more than a contact card.

The live page gives existing clients a shortcut. A new visitor gets very little visible context before choosing whether to make contact.

Website experience01

A contact page is doing the whole job.

The homepage we saw on October 20 showed contact details, booking buttons and policies, without a visible practitioner introduction, treatment navigation or office hours.

Live homepage / visible-page review ↗

Why a customer might hesitateA new visitor may leave before making contact if they cannot quickly understand who will see them, what the practice offers or whether it fits their needs.

How we’d fix it

Build a concise homepage with a practitioner introduction, priority treatment paths, location hours and approved proof. Confirm whether the current page is an interim rebuild before replacing anything.

New-client handoff02

A form starts the relationship. Then what?

The new-client button points to a Google Form; returning clients are sent to Boulevard. The homepage does not set an expected response time for a new inquiry. This is a different path, not proof that the form fails.

New-client destination linked from homepage ↗

Why a customer might hesitateSomeone ready to inquire may hesitate when they cannot tell when they will hear back or what happens after submitting the form.

How we’d fix it

Give new clients a branded consultation page, explain the response window, and connect the approved next step to the clinic’s actual scheduling process. Test the confirmation and staff handoff.

Contact accuracy03

One email label. Another destination.

The visible contact address is contact@angellmedicalspa.com, while its email link opens schedule@angellmedicalspa.com. Both may be valid, but the mismatch makes the intended inbox unclear.

Homepage email link / observed October 20 ↗

Why a customer might hesitateVisitors should not have to guess which inbox to use. Conflicting labels can make a simple first contact feel less certain.

How we’d fix it

Confirm which inbox the team monitors, then align the displayed address and email link. Add a clear purpose for scheduling versus general questions.

The first screen

The large logo leads the desktop page while key details sit farther down. Bring a useful introduction and the new-client route into the first screen.

Visible treatment context

Give each priority treatment a useful, approved page that explains the service and links to the right consultation step.

Practice story

Introduce Dr. Angell and the team in plain language, with current roles and credentials confirmed by the practice before publication.

Mobile loading: a measured starting point

One October 20 PageSpeed mobile lab run returned performance 75/100 and a 4.5-second main-content loading time (Largest Contentful Paint). The run used a simulated Moto G Power phone and slow 4G connection; no real-visitor field data was available.

PageSpeed mobile report ↗

Vessel would: Prioritize the report’s files that delay the first view and image delivery, then retest under the same conditions. This is one controlled run, not a claim about every visitor.

Existing social content

The public @angellmedicalspa Instagram profile includes video content. Public profile inspection did not establish a reliable latest-post date; sign-in restricted the post-detail checks. This is not evidence of inactivity.

Official Instagram profile ↗

Vessel would: Build on the existing voice. Review a dated content sample with account access, then connect the strongest practitioner explanations to the relevant service pages.

What the public ad library showed

Selected Meta advertiser ↗

Website measurement is already present

Website script review ↗

Search + AI discovery
