02 / The opportunityPublic-page review / October 20, 2026

A first-time visitor needs more than a contact card.

The live page gives existing clients a shortcut. A new visitor gets very little visible context before choosing whether to make contact.

Website experience01

A contact page is doing the whole job.

The rendered homepage in our October 20 browser review showed contact details, booking buttons and policies, without a visible practitioner introduction, treatment navigation or office hours. Search extraction still surfaced an older service paragraph, so the visible experience and indexed text differ.

Live homepage / visible-page review ↗
Vessel would

Build a concise homepage with a practitioner introduction, priority treatment paths, location hours and approved proof. Confirm whether the current page is an interim rebuild before replacing anything.

New-client handoff02

A form starts the relationship. Then what?

The new-client button points to a Google Form; returning clients are sent to Boulevard. The homepage does not set an expected response time for a new inquiry. This is a different path, not proof that the form fails.

New-client destination linked from homepage ↗
Vessel would

Give new clients a branded consultation page, explain the response window, and connect the approved next step to the clinic’s actual scheduling process. Test the confirmation and staff handoff.

Contact accuracy03

One email label. Another destination.

The visible contact address is contact@angellmedicalspa.com, while its email link opens schedule@angellmedicalspa.com. Both may be valid, but the mismatch makes the intended inbox unclear.

Homepage email link / observed October 20 ↗
Vessel would

Confirm which inbox the team monitors, then align the displayed address and email link. Add a clear purpose for scheduling versus general questions.

First-screen hierarchy

The large logo leads the desktop page while key details sit farther down. Bring a useful introduction and the new-client route into the first screen.

Visible treatment context

Build approved, service-specific pages rather than relying on a broad paragraph in search extraction. Link each to a relevant consultation step.

Practice story

Introduce Dr. Angell and the team in plain language, with current roles and credentials confirmed by the practice before publication.
